# Heritage & Technical Research

This directory contains foundational research data, historical sources, archaeological references, and technical feasibility studies conducted for **SAARTHI**.

## 📚 Structure

- **`Research Data/`**: Compiled statistical datasets, visitor behavior studies, accessibility audits, and monument spatial data.
- **`Research Sources & References/`**: Citations from Archaeological Survey of India (ASI), UNESCO World Heritage Centre dossiers, academic historical publications, and 3D photogrammetry benchmarks.
